Extended Events viewer — aggregates under their columns (plan-xevents-followups.md W4)

Captured from the built binary, win10cli (17), a throwaway session's event_file:
grouped by name then database_name, SUM and MAX of duration. Every number
matches the server's own GROUP BY over the same file.

Rule chosen: an aggregate goes under its column unless that column is hidden
or is the first column (then it stays in the label, "SUM(duration) = 125").
The label spills across empty cells and is clipped with "…" at the first
non-empty cell on screen.
